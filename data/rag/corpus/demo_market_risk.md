# Illustrative Market Risk Framework

Synthetic educational content for testing the retrieval pipeline.

Annualized volatility summarizes variability of returns. Maximum drawdown measures the largest peak-to-trough decline over an observation window. Historical Value at Risk at 95 percent estimates a loss threshold from the observed return distribution; Expected Shortfall summarizes average loss beyond that threshold. These measures describe different aspects of risk and are not interchangeable.

Financial NLP signals such as sentiment and event labels are noisy. A positive headline does not guarantee positive returns. Store source, timestamp, entity, and model version. Evaluate model outputs on chronological holdout data and never present them as guaranteed outcomes.
